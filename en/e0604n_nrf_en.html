Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa

Homage to the Blessed One, the Arahant, the Perfectly Self-Enlightened One.

Suttavandanā

Homage to the Discourses

1.

1.

Dhammattha [M 0.0013] desanā vedha,

Catugambhīra duddasaṃ;

Dhammaṃ lokassa desentaṃ,

Dhammarājaṃ namāmahaṃ;

The teaching of the Dhamma's meaning is penetrating, fourfold deep, and difficult to see; I pay homage to the King of Dhamma, who teaches the Dhamma to the world.

2.

2.

Buddho [M 0.0014] paccekabuddho ca,

Arahā cakkavattīti;

Thūpāthūpārahaṃ vande,

Catuthūpārahesu taṃ;

The Buddha, the Paccekabuddha, the Arahant, and the Wheel-turning Monarch—these are worthy of a stupa. Among these four who are worthy of a stupa, I venerate him.

3.

3.

Petaseyyo [M 0.0015] kāmabhogī,

Sīhaseyyo tathāgato;

Sayantaṃ catuseyyesu,

Catutthena name jinaṃ;

The sensual enjoyer lies down like a departed spirit; the Tathāgata lies down like a lion. Among the four ways of lying down, I bow to the Conqueror, who adopts the fourth.

4.

4.

Aṇḍajā jalābujā ca;

Saṃsedajo papātikā;

Catuyoni pariccheda,

Ñāṇavantaṃ name jinaṃ;

Egg-born and womb-born, moisture-born and spontaneously arisen—I bow to the wise Conqueror, who has comprehended the four modes of birth.

5.

5.

Sutabuddhonubuddho [M 0.0016] ca,

Pacceko jinabuddhoti;

Catutthaṃ catubuddhesu,

Vandāmi lokanāyakaṃ;

The one enlightened through hearing, the one enlightened through realization, the Individually Enlightened One, and the Conquering Buddha—I venerate the fourth among these four Buddhas, the Leader of the World.

6.

6.

Rāhuggaṃ [M 0.0017] attabhāgīnaṃ,

Mandhātā kāmabhogīnaṃ;

Māro adhipateyyānaṃ,

Buddho loke sadevake;

Catuagga paññattīnaṃ,

Aggapattaṃ name jinaṃ.

Rāhu is foremost among those with a physical frame, Mandhātā among sensual enjoyers, Māra among those with sovereignty, and the Buddha in the world with its gods. Among these four supreme designations, I bow to the Conqueror who has attained the highest.

7.

7.

Dānaṃ [M 0.0018] piyakathā attha,

Cariyā sadisattatā;

Catusaṅgaha vatthūhi,

Asaṅkhiyesu jātisu;

Lokassa saṅgahetāraṃ,

Vandāmi lokanāyakaṃ;

Giving, kindly speech, beneficial conduct, and impartiality; through these four bases of sympathy, in countless births, he gathered the world. I venerate that Leader of the World.

8.

8.

Lobho doso ca mohoti,

Anto paccatthike tayo;

Dujjaye [M 0.0019] sabbaverīhi,

Sañjitaṃpi sudhiṃ name;

Greed, hatred, and delusion—these three are the inner adversaries. I bow to the wise one who has conquered these, so difficult to conquer for all other foes.

9.

9.

Jāti puñña mahattāni,

Guṇamahattamuttamaṃ;

Timahatthehi sampannaṃ,

Vandāmi lokanāyakaṃ;

Greatness of birth, greatness of merit, and the supreme greatness of virtue—I venerate the Leader of the World, who is endowed with these three greatnesses.

10.

10.

Hetu [M 0.0020] phala sampadāyo,

Sattupakārasampadā;

Tisampadāhi sampannaṃ,

Vandāmi lokanāyakaṃ.

The perfection of the cause, the perfection of the fruit, and the perfection of benefiting beings—I venerate the Leader of the World, who is endowed with these three perfections.

11.

11.

Ñāṇappahānānubhāva,

Surūpakārasampadā;

Catuphala sampadāhi,

Taṃ sampannaṃ name jinaṃ.

Perfection of knowledge, of abandoning, of power, and of beautiful form—I bow to the accomplished Conqueror, endowed with these four perfections of the fruit.

12.

12.

Āsayo [M 0.0021] ca payogoti,

Sattupakāra sampadā;

Duvidhā yassa sampannā,

Sammā maṃ pātu so jino.

Inclination and application: the twofold perfection of benefiting beings. He who is endowed with these two, may that Conqueror rightly protect me.

13.

13.

Mahosadhe vessantare,

Pacchimabhavike tathā;

Tijātīsu name vācaṃ,

Bhāsentaṃ jāyanakkhaṇe.

As Mahosadha, as Vessantara, and also in his final existence—I bow to him who uttered speech at the moment of his birth in these three lifetimes.

14.

14.

Nemimhi [M 0.0022] sādhine ceva,

Mandhātari ca guttile;

Catūsu manujattena,

Devalokaṃ gataṃ name.

As Nemi, Sādhina, Mandhātā, and Guttila—I bow to him who, in these four lives, went to the world of the devas in a human state.

15.

15.

Saṅkhāro rūpavikāro,

Lakkhaṇaṃ vānanissaṭaṃ;

Nibbānaṃ [M 0.0023] paññatticeti,

Pañca ñeyyantaguṃ name.

Formations, the alteration of form, characteristics, Nibbāna which is freedom from craving, and concepts—I bow to him who has gone to the end of the five knowables.

16.

16.

Putta dāra pariccāgā,

Rajjaṅgaṃ jīva cāgīti;

Pañca mahāpariccāge,

Karaṃ sudukkare name.

Relinquishing son, wife, kingdom, limbs, and life—I bow to him who made these five great relinquishments, so extremely difficult to perform.

17.

17.

Rūpañca [M 0.0024] vedanā saññā,

Saṅkhārā viññāṇantime;

Pañcakkhandhe parijānaṃ,

Tipariññāhi taṃ name.

Form, feeling, perception, volitional formations, and consciousness—I bow to him who fully understood the five aggregates with the threefold full understanding.

18.

18.

Manussa deva gatiyo,

Peto ca nirayo tathā;

Tiracchānoti pañcetā,

Chindantaṃ gatiyo name;

The human and deva destinies, the ghost realm, hell, and the animal realm—I bow to him who cuts off these five destinies.

19.

19.

Devaputto [M 0.0025] kileso ca,

Abhisaṅkhāra mārako;

Khandho maccūti pañcete,

Māre vijitavaṃ name.

Māra as the deva's son, as defilement, as volitional formations, as the aggregates, and as death—I bow to him who has conquered these five Māras.

20.

20.

Purebhattaṃ pacchābhattaṃ,

Purimañceva majjhimaṃ;

Pacchimanti sadā pañca,

Buddhakiccaṃ karaṃ name.

In the forenoon, in the afternoon, in the first watch of the night, the middle watch, and the last watch—I bow to him who always performs the fivefold duties of a Buddha.

21.

21.

Rūpā [M 0.0026] saddā gandhā rasā,

Phoṭṭhabbā ca manoramā;

Pañca kāmaguṇe hitvā,

Kheḷaṃva niggataṃ name.

Delightful forms, sounds, scents, tastes, and tactile objects—having abandoned these five strands of sensual pleasure, I bow to him who cast them out like spittle.

22.

22.

Rāgasallaṃ [M 0.0027] dosasallaṃ,

Mohā māno ca diṭṭhīti;

Vande vajjātivajjantaṃ,

Pañcasallapanūdanaṃ.

The arrow of lust, the arrow of hatred, the arrows of delusion, conceit, and views—I venerate the dispeller of the five arrows, who has transcended all that is faulty.

23.

23.

Dīgho majjhima saṃyuttā,

Aṅguttaro ca khuddako;

Iti pañca nikāyehi,

Suvinentaṃ pajaṃ name.

The Long Discourses, the Middle Length Discourses, the Connected Discourses, the Numerical Discourses, and the Collection of Minor Texts—I bow to him who, with these five Nikāyas, perfectly trains all beings.

24.

24.

Issariyaṃ [M 0.0028] yaso dhammo,

Kāmo sirī payattīti;

Bhagyehi chahi sampannaṃ,

Vandāmi lokanāyakaṃ.

Sovereignty, fame, righteousness, fulfillment of wishes, glory, and diligence—I venerate the Leader of the World, who is endowed with these six fortunes.

25.

25.

Piyāpiyaṃ [M 0.0029] bhūtābhūtaṃ,

Atthānatthaṃ yathārahaṃ;

Missā chasu suvācaṃva,

Bhāsantaṃ atthakaṃ name.

Whether agreeable or disagreeable, true or untrue, beneficial or unbeneficial—I bow to him who speaks only what is true and beneficial, at the proper time, from among these six kinds of speech.

26.

26.

Alaso ca pamādo ca,

Anuṭṭhānaṃ asaṃyamo;

Niddā tantīti chiddehi,

Chahi muttaṃ sadā name.

Sloth and heedlessness, lack of exertion and lack of restraint, sleep and drowsiness—I always bow to him who is freed from these six faults.

27.

27.

Mahākaruṇāsamāpatti[M 0.0030],

Yamakappāṭihāriyaṃ;

Āsayānusaye ñāṇaṃ,

Indriyāna paropare;

Sabbaññutā nāvaraṇaṃ,

Chā sādhāraṇikaṃ name.

Knowledge of the superior and inferior faculties of beings; knowledge of their inclinations and underlying tendencies; the Twin Miracle; the attainment of great compassion; omniscience; and unobstructed knowledge—I bow to him endowed with these six uncommon qualities.

28.

28.

Kāmacchando [M 0.0031] ca byāpādo,

Thinamiddhañca saṃsayo;

Avijjuddhacca kukkuccaṃ,

Chavinīvaraṇaṃ name.

Sensual desire, ill will, sloth and torpor, doubt, restlessness and remorse, and ignorance—I bow to him who has overcome these six hindrances.

29.

29.

Iddhividhaṃ dibbasotaṃ,

Paracitta vijānanā;

Pubbenivāsānussati[M 0.0032],

Dibbacakkhāsavakkhayo;

Chaḷabhiññāhi sampannaṃ,

Vandāmi purisuttamaṃ.

The kinds of psychic power, the divine ear, knowledge of the minds of others, recollection of past lives, the divine eye, and the destruction of the taints—I venerate the Supreme Person, who is endowed with these six higher knowledges.

30.

30.

Nīla pītā ca odātā,

Mañjiṭṭhā ca pabhassarā;

Lohitāti cha raṃsīhī,

Vijjotantaṃ sadā name.

Blue, yellow, white, crimson, radiant, and red—I always bow to him who shines forth with these six rays.

31.

31.

Assādādīnavo [M 0.0033] ceva,

Nissaraṇaphalaṃ tathā;

Upāyāṇattīti chadhā,

Saddhammadesakaṃ name.

Gratification and drawback, escape and fruit, the means and the proclamation—I bow to the Teacher of the True Dhamma, who teaches in these six ways.

32.

32.

Rāgo [M 0.0034] doso ca moho ca,

Vitakkacariyā tathā;

Saddhā buddhīti bhājentaṃ,

Tā chaḷecariyā name.

The lustful, hateful, and deluded temperaments; the speculative, faithful, and intelligent temperaments—I bow to him who analyzes these six kinds of temperament.

33.

33.

Rajataṃ kanakaṃ muttā,

Maṇi veḷuriyāni ca;

Vajirañca pavāḷanti,

Seṭṭhi sattadhano viya.

Silver, gold, pearls, jewels, beryl, diamonds, and coral—like a great merchant with these seven kinds of wealth.

34.

34.

Saddhāsīlaṃ sutaṃcāgo,

Hirī ottappiyaṃ dhanaṃ,

Paññādhananti sambuddhaṃ,

Vande sattamahādhanaṃ.

Faith, virtue, learning, generosity, moral shame, moral dread, and wisdom—I venerate the Perfectly Self-Enlightened One, who possesses these seven great treasures.

35.

35.

Cakkaṃ [M 0.0035] hatthi asso maṇi,

Gahapatitthiyo tathā;

Supariṇāyakocāti,

Sattaratano cakkavattīva.

The wheel, elephant, horse, jewel, householder, woman, and counselor—like a wheel-turning monarch with his seven treasures.

36.

36.

Sati dhammavicayo ca,

Tatheva vīriyaṃ pīti;

Passaddhi [M 0.0036] samādhupekkhā,

Saddhamma cakkavattikaṃ;

Satta bojjhaṅgaratanaṃ,

Namāmi purisuttamaṃ.

Mindfulness, investigation of phenomena, energy, rapture, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity—I bow to the Supreme Person, the Wheel-turning Monarch of the True Dhamma, who possesses these seven treasures of enlightenment.

37.

37.

Yugandharo [M 0.0037] īsadharo,

Karavīko sudassano;

Nemindharo vinatako,

Assakaṇṇo mahānage;

Sattete hattharūpaṃva,

Sampassantaṃ jinaṃ name.

Yugandhara, Īsadhara, Karavīka, Sudassana, Nemindhara, Vinataka, and Assakaṇṇa—I bow to the Conqueror who clearly sees these seven great mountains.

38.

38.

Kaṇṇamuṇḍo anotatto,

Kuṇālo rathakārako;

Sīhappapāta chaddantā,

Mandākinī mahāsare;

Sattete [M 0.0038] sabbathā passaṃ,

Diṭṭheva pātikaṃ name.

Kaṇṇamuṇḍa, Anotatta, Kuṇāla, Rathakāraka, Sīhappapāta, Chaddanta, and Mandākinī—I bow to him who has directly seen all seven of these great lakes.

39.

39.

Manussattaṃ liṅgasampatti,

Hetu satthāradassanaṃ;

Pabbajjā guṇasampatti,

Adhikāro ca chandatā;

Aṭṭhadhamma samodhānā,

Bhinīhārantaguṃ name.

Human birth, perfection of gender, the right cause, seeing the Teacher, renunciation, attainment of special qualities, a great service, and strong aspiration—I bow to him who, with these eight conditions fulfilled, made his great aspiration.

40.

40.

Visaṭṭhaṃ [M 0.0039] mañju viññeyyaṃ,

Savanīyāvisāriṇā;

Gambhīro binduninnādo,

Aṭṭhaṅgikasaraṃ name.

Clear, melodious, intelligible, pleasing to the ear, non-diffuse, deep, resonant, and sonorous—I bow to him who possesses the voice with these eight qualities.

41.

41.

Cattāri [M 0.0040] rūpajhānāni,

Tathevārūpajhānāni;

Tā samāpattiyo aṭṭha,

Sevantaṃ ruciyā name.

The four form jhānas and likewise the four formless jhānas—I bow to him who, with delight, cultivates these eight attainments.

42.

42.

Aṇimā laṅghimā kammaṃ,

Mahimā pattimā tathā;

Īsitā [M 0.0041] vasitā ceva,

Yatthakāmāvasāyitā;

Aṭṭhissariya puṇṇattā,

Issarātissaraṃ name.

The power to become minute, light, or great; the power of attainment; the power of irresistible will; the powers of lordship, control, and accomplishing whatever is desired—I bow to the Lord of Lords, who is complete in these eight masteries.

43.

43.

Manomayiddhiñāṇañca[M 0.0042],

Chaḷabhiññā vipassanā;

Aṭṭhavijjāhi sampannaṃ,

Vande tilokaketukaṃ.

The power of creating a mind-made body, the six higher knowledges, and insight knowledge—I venerate the Banner of the Three Worlds, who is endowed with these eight kinds of knowledge.

44.

44.

Jāti jarā rujā kālo,

Caturāpāyadukkhāti;

Aṭṭha saṃvegavatthūni,

Bhāsantaṃ vividhaṃ name.

Birth, aging, sickness, and death, and the suffering in the four woeful states—I bow to him who proclaims in various ways these eight grounds for spiritual urgency.

45.

45.

Suttaṅgaṃ [M 0.0043] veyyākaraṇaṃ,

Geyyaṃ gāthā ca jātakaṃ;

Abbhūtadhamma vedallā,

Udānamiti vuttakaṃ;

Navaṅgaṃ sabbalokassa,

Dadantaṃ sāsanaṃ name.

Discourses, explanations, mixed prose and verse, verses, birth stories, wondrous phenomena, catechetical discourses, inspired utterances, and sayings—I bow to him who gave the nine-factored Dispensation to the entire world.

46.

46.

Yā [M 0.0044] samāpattiyo aṭṭha,

Nirodho cāti te nava,

Anupubbavihāre taṃ,

Sevantaṃ ruciyā name.

The eight attainments and the attainment of cessation—I bow to him who, with delight, cultivates these nine successive abidings.

47.

47.

Ekattañceva nānattaṃ,

Kāyasaññīhi yogato,

Cattāro tathāruppā ca,

Asaññīcāti te nava,

Sattavāse vibhajitvā,

Pakāsentaṃ name jinaṃ.

Beings with diversity and unity in body and perception, the four formless realms, and the non-percipient beings—I bow to the Conqueror who analyzed and proclaimed these nine abodes of beings.

48.

48.

Seyyassa [M 0.0045] sadiso seyyo,

Hīnoti tividho vidho,

Tathā sadisahīnānaṃ,

Navavidhe nudaṃ name.

The threefold classification of 'better,' 'equal,' and 'inferior'—I bow to him who dispels the ninefold conceit that arises from such comparisons.

49.

49.

Anatthaṃ [M 0.0046] me carati ca,

Tathā cari carissati,

Mittassa arinotvatthaṃ,

Navāghāte jahaṃ name.

'He has harmed me, is harming me, will harm me'; and likewise for one's friend and one's enemy—I bow to him who has abandoned these nine grounds for resentment.

50.

50.

Dānaṃ sīlañca nikkhamaṃ,

Paññā vīriyapañcamaṃ,

Khantī [M 0.0047] saccamadhiṭṭhānaṃ,

Mettupekkhāti tādasa,

Pūretvā pāramī vande,

Sammāsambodhimajjhavaṃ;

Generosity, virtue, and renunciation; wisdom, and energy as the fifth; patience, truthfulness, determination, loving-kindness, and equanimity—I venerate him who, having fulfilled these ten perfections, attained Perfect Self-Enlightenment.

51.

51.

Kāḷāvakañca gaṅgeyyaṃ,

Paṇḍaraṃ tamba piṅgalaṃ,

Gandha maṅgala hemañca,

Uposatha chaddantime.

Kāḷāvaka, Gaṅgeyya, Paṇḍara, Tamba, Piṅgala, Gandha, Maṅgala, Hema, Uposatha, and Chaddanta.

52.

52.

Dhāreti dasaposānaṃ,

Nāgo kāḷāvako balaṃ;

Sahassakoṭiposānaṃ[M 0.0048];

Chaddantovāranuttamo.

A Kāḷāvaka elephant has the strength of ten men. The supreme elephant Chaddanta has the strength of a thousand koṭis of men.

53.

53.

Dasa sahassakoṭīnaṃ,

Posānaṃ baladhāraṇaṃ;

Chaddantānaṃ dasannañca,

Dasakāyabalaṃ name.

His physical strength is that of ten Chaddanta elephants, equivalent to ten thousand koṭis of men. I bow to him who possesses this bodily strength of ten elephants.

54.

54.

Ṭhānāṭhāne [M 0.0049] vipāke ca,

Magge sabbatthagāminaṃ;

Nānādhātūsu lokesu,

Adhimuttimhi pāṇinaṃ;

Knowledge of the possible as possible and the impossible as impossible; knowledge of the results of kamma; knowledge of the path leading to all destinations; knowledge of the world with its many and diverse elements; knowledge of the diverse inclinations of beings;

55.

55.

Paropariyatte ñāṇaṃ,

Indriyānañca pāṇinaṃ;

Jhānādīsu ñāṇaṃ pubbe,

Nivāse dibbacakkhu ca;

Āsavakkhayañāṇanti,

Dasabalañāṇaṃ name.

Knowledge of the state of the faculties of other beings; knowledge of the defilement, cleansing, and emergence regarding the jhānas and other attainments; recollection of past lives; the divine eye; and knowledge of the destruction of the taints—I bow to him who possesses these ten powers of a Tathāgata.

56.

56.

Mūlagandho [M 0.0050] sāro pheggu,

Taco papaṭiko raso;

Pattaṃ pupphaṃ phalañceva,

Gandhagandhoti saṃvaraṃ;

Sugandhaṃ dasagandhehi,

Vilimpantaṃ sadā name.

The fragrance of root, heartwood, sapwood, bark, outer bark, sap, leaf, flower, and fruit, and the supreme fragrance of virtue—I always bow to him who is anointed with these ten fragrances.

57.

57.

Samādhi [M 0.0051] ñāṇavipphārā,

Adhiṭṭhānaṃ vikubbanā;

Manomayāriyā iddhi,

Tathā kammavipākajā.

The psychic powers of pervasive concentration and pervasive knowledge; the powers of determination and transformation; mind-made power, noble power, and power born of the result of kamma;

58.

58.

Vijjāmayā puññavato;

Payoga paccayiddhiti;

Aggapatta dasiddhīnaṃ,

Pāṭiheraṃ karaṃ name.

The power produced by knowledge, the power of the meritorious, and the power conditioned by effort—I bow to the performer of miracles, who has attained the highest of these ten psychic powers.

59.

59.

Muttā [M 0.0052] maṇi veḷuriyaṃ,

Saṅkho sallo pavāḷakaṃ;

Suvaṇṇaṃ rajataṃ lohi,

Taṅko masāragallanti;

Dasadhā [M 0.0053] ratanehiggaṃ,

Bhogiṃ paññāmaṇiṃ name.

Pearl, jewel, beryl, conch, crystal, coral, gold, silver, ruby, and cat's-eye—I bow to him who possesses the supreme jewel of wisdom, surpassing these ten treasures.

60.

60.

Sithilaṃ dhanitaṃ dīghaṃ,

Rassañca garukaṃ lahu;

Niggahitaṃ vimuttañca,

Sambandhañca vavatthitaṃ;

Vivarantaṃ name dhammaṃ,

Dasabyañjanabheditaṃ.

Lax and aspirated, long and short, heavy and light, checked and released, connected and separated—I bow to the Dhamma, which is analyzed with these ten phonetic features.

61.

61.

Pasādojo [M 0.0054] madhuratā,

Samatā sukhumālatā;

Silesodāratā kanti,

Atthabyatti samādhayo;

Dasa saddaguṇopetaṃ,

Dhammaṃ pātu karaṃ name.

Clarity, vigor, sweetness, evenness, and subtlety; cohesion, loftiness, and loveliness; clarity of meaning and concentration—I pay homage, making manifest the Dhamma endowed with these ten qualities of speech.

62.

62.

Lobho [M 0.0055] doso moho māno,

Uddhaccaṃ diṭṭhi saṃsayo;

Ahirikamanottappaṃ,

Thinanti sabba dāhake;

Kilese dasa te saddhiṃ,

Vāsanāya jahaṃ name.

Greed, hatred, delusion, conceit, restlessness, wrong view, doubt, shamelessness, lack of moral dread, and sloth—I pay homage to the abandoning of these ten scorching defilements along with their latent tendencies.

63.

63.

Saterā [M 0.0056] daṇḍamaṇikā,

Macchavilolagaggarā;

Sukkhāsani kapisisā,

Vicakkā sañña kukkuṭā;

Navāsanīhi phālentaṃ,

Tibbaṃ paññāsaniṃ name.

The razor-wheel with spokes, the fish-agitated cauldron, the dry stake, the monkey-headed torture, the turning wheel, the crow-snare; I pay homage to the fierce thunderbolt of wisdom, which strikes with nine thunderbolts.

64.

64.

Rodanā [M 0.0057] kodhāvudho ca,

Ujjhantissariyaṃ tathā;

Paṭisaṅkhānaṃ,

Khanti aṭṭhabalesu taṃ;

Pathavīsadisaṃ vande,

Aṭṭhamena vināyakaṃ.

Weeping, the weapon of anger, grumbling, and likewise dominance; reflection and patience are among the eight powers. I venerate the Leader, who is like the earth, and is without the eighth of these.

65.

65.

Kāmarogo [M 0.0058] ca paṭigho,

Māno diṭṭhi ca saṃsayo;

Bhavarāgo avijjāti,

Sattānusayinaṃ name.

Sensual lust, aversion, conceit, wrong view, doubt, craving for existence, and ignorance—I pay homage to the abandoning of these seven latent tendencies.

66.

66.

Titthiyāpakatimahāsāvakā,

Aggasāvakā paccekabuddhasambuddhā;

Visiṭṭhaṃ [M 0.0059] sabbathā chasu,

Pubbe nivāsa viññūsu;

Tilokamakuṭaṃ name.
