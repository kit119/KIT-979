Pamāṇaṃ hoti;

Just as a carpenter’s line is a measure, so too is this a measure for the discerning.

This is the nature of things, this is its intrinsic nature, this is the fixed order, it is said.

I constantly revere the Triple Gem; to the Teacher, I always bow; “May all blessings be mine everywhere.”

Pālito [M 0.0592] pāḷiyā cheko,

Tvaṃsi gambhīra-ñāṇavā;

Pāliyāva pālitassa,

Dadāmidāni bho ahaṃ.

Pālita, skilled in Pāli, you are indeed profound in wisdom. To Pālita, protected by Pāli, I now offer this, sir.

2.

2.

Dakkhāvādesu [M 0.0593] kusalo,

Pālito sāsanandharī;

Piṭakesu ajjhogāyha,

Naradakkhaṃbhisaṅkharī.

Skilled in eloquent speech, Pālita, upholder of the Teaching, having deeply immersed himself in the Piṭakas, composed the Naradakkha.

3.

3.

Suta-dharena [M 0.0595] racitaṃ,

Etaṃ sāra-gavesino;

Atandikā su-dakkhantu,

Aggagga-sāsane ratā.

Composed by one rich in learning, this is for seekers of the essence. May the diligent, those delighting in the supreme Teaching, understand it well.

Ñuṃ [M 0.0596] pālitodha jāto yo,

Thero so abbhutova ñuṃ;

Ñuṃ mahā-pālito santo,

Nikāya-pālito ca ñuṃ.

He who was born here as Pālita, that elder was truly wondrous; now the great Pālita, serene, and guardian of the Nikāyas.

2.

2.

Ñuṃ [M 0.0598] nara-dakkha-ganthaṃ yaṃ,

Sovakā nara-dakkha-daṃ;

Narā dakkhantu sammā ca,

Dakkhattaṃ pāpuṇantu ñuṃ.

Now, this text of Naradakkha gives skill to people; may people perceive it rightly and attain true skill.
